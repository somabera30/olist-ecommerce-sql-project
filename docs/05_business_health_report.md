# Executive Business Health Report

## Objective

This report provides a view of the Olist marketplace using key business metrics.

The purpose is to establish a baseline understanding of the business and identify an area for deeper analysis.

---

---

# Key Observations

- Olist recorded **13.22 million** in product sales value across **96,478 delivered orders**, supported by **2,970 active sellers** and more than **110,000 items sold**. This shows a marketplace with substantial transaction activity during the period covered by the dataset.

- The marketplace served **93,358 unique customers** across **96,478 delivered orders**, or approximately **1.03 delivered orders per customer** on average. This does not tell us the actual repeat customer rate, but it raises an important question: how much of the business comes from repeat customers versus one-time buyers?

- Customers waited an average of **12.56 days** for delivery and gave delivered orders an average rating of **4.2 / 5**. These metrics provide a useful baseline for customer experience and may become relevant when investigating what influences repeat purchasing.

---

# Focus for Next Analysis

The comparison between delivered orders and unique customers raises an important business question:

> **How can Olist improve customer retention and repeat purchasing?**

A marketplace needs not only to attract customers, but also to encourage them to return and purchase again.

The current health metrics show a large customer base, but the relationship between total customers and delivered orders raises a question about the strength of repeat purchasing. Understanding this is important because repeat customers can contribute to sustained business activity over time.

Therefore, the next analysis will focus on:

- How many customers make repeat purchases.
- How often customers return.
- When repeat purchases happen.
- Which factors are associated with repeat purchasing.
- What actions could help improve customer retention.

---

## Conclusion

These initial metrics provide a baseline view of Olist's marketplace activity, customer experience, and operational performance.

The findings suggest that **customer retention and repeat purchasing are a promising area for deeper analysis**. The next stage will investigate repeat purchasing behavior to determine whether it represents a meaningful business opportunity and identify potential actions Olist could take to improve retention.
